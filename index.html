How can we help?

Scheduling & Patient Support

Scheduling, confirmations, and rescheduling · patient intake and onboarding · live provider support through the day. Your front desk, fully covered — without the hiring, turnover, or retraining.

We keep your day-to-day running smoothly and look for ways to make it better as we go. We follow your existing workflows for scheduling and confirmations — then refine them, adding the small improvements and automations that remove friction over time.

We can handle your phones completely, so no call goes unanswered and no patient is left waiting.

And we make sure patients don't fall through the cracks. We can set up scheduled outreach, quarterly recall lists, and a strategic approach to keeping your schedule full — and when patients do drop off, we can look into why and report back, so you can see what's actually happening in your practice.

What this looks like

Insurance & Authorizations

The work that decides whether you get paid.

Prior authorization submission and follow-up · insurance coordination · eligibility and benefits verification · patient financial clarity. Experienced PA specialists who don't just submit and wait — they work to get approvals across the line.

Our prior authorization specialists do more than file paperwork. They advise providers on the note improvements that strengthen an approval, keep your staff current on insurance guidelines, and follow up on every submission until there's a decision — no auth left sitting in limbo.

Eligibility and benefits are verified before the visit, so coverage problems — an inactive plan, a required auth, a changed policy — surface early, not at check-in. Patients know their copay and responsibility before they arrive, which means fewer disputes, a faster front desk, and no surprises.

Where a practice wants it, we can also manage patient balance follow-up — set up to your policies, working through your systems, so responsibility is handled consistently and respectfully without pulling your staff off other work.

What this looks like

Systems & Automation

Less manual work. Clearer numbers.

Workflow mapping and optimization · automation of repetitive tasks · data cleanup and accurate entry · reporting and dashboards · system connections and migration.

We start by mapping how work actually moves through your practice — then strip out the repetitive steps, automating what's worth automating and leaving the rest clean and simple. The goal is less manual work for your staff, not technology for its own sake.

We connect the tools you already use so information passes between them instead of being retyped, and we clean up the data you already have — deduping records, fixing inconsistencies, standardizing how things are entered — so what's in your system can be trusted.

And we turn that data into something you can actually read. Clear reporting on the numbers that matter — no-show rates, schedule gaps, authorization turnaround, patient volume — so you can see how your practice is really running, not guess at it.

We also document the workflows we manage, so the way things are done is written down and repeatable — never living in one person's head.

Compliance isn't a feature. It's the foundation.

We operate as a HIPAA business associate, with a signed Business Associate Agreement in place for every client before any patient information is touched.

How we protect your patients' information:

We work inside your systems. Wherever possible, patient information stays in your EHR and platforms — we minimize whatever moves outside them.

Controlled, encrypted, minimal access. Team members work through secure, company-managed environments, with access limited to only what their role requires.

Every team member is HIPAA-trained before touching a client account.

A written security program governs how we handle, access, and protect information — built to HIPAA and to U.S. state data-security standards, including Massachusetts 201 CMR 17.00, one of the strictest in the country.

If you operate under your own compliance requirements, we adapt to them. The standard is yours to set; meeting it is ours.
